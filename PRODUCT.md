# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Founders with an idea or an early-stage startup, owners of growing and established businesses, and content creators. They arrive wanting a partner that can help them build a brand, launch, reach customers or grow a creator presence, and they are deciding whether Trayang is the right team to start a conversation with.

## Product Purpose
Trayang is a strategy, branding, marketing and business-consulting studio. The site exists to make a visitor understand what Trayang does, trust how it thinks and works, and send an enquiry. Success is a qualified enquiry that states the goal, support needed, timeline and approximate budget.

## Positioning
One team connecting business consulting, startup support, branding, marketing, creator management and events: strategy, creativity and execution working together instead of disconnected deliverables. "Creative with a commercial purpose."

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Six service pillars: Marketing; Branding & Creative; Business Consulting; Startup Support; Influencer & Creator Management; Events & Activations.
- Ten detailed capabilities (Business & Brand Strategy through Influencer & Creator Management), listed in data/site.ts.
- Engagements are projects or ongoing partnerships; timelines, pricing and results are never promised on the site.
- Enquiries: an enquiry form that composes an email to hello@trayang.com (no backend).
- The home page is a scroll-jacked story (lib/scrollStory.ts) that must keep its stage choreography and pacing.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: TRAYANG. Sign-off line: "TRAYANG · RISE BEYOND". Motto: "Think clearly. Build purposefully. Move forward."
- Palette is fixed by the client: brown #3B2112, gold #D89B52, light gold #F0C27A, cream #E8DDC5, near-black #0B1012.
- Voice: plain, confident, realistic. Sets expectations without promising timelines, results or capabilities that have not been confirmed.

## Evidence on Hand
- Copy for home, about, services, contact, FAQ, terms and privacy supplied by the client (October 2026).
- Imagery: public/images/artisan-studio.webp (team at a moodboard table), gift-service.webp (gold growth bars on a desk), hero-lifestyle.webp (3D gold brand/marketing still life).
- No client names, logos, case studies, testimonials, statistics or social profiles yet. Hidden slots exist for them; never fabricate any.

## Product Principles
- Clarity before cleverness: every section answers what Trayang does or how working together begins.
- Show the joined-up system, not a list of disconnected services.
- Honest expectations: no invented proof, numbers or guarantees.
- Every page ends with one clear next step: the enquiry.
