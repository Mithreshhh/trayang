## Scope

Whole Trayang marketing site: the scroll-jacked home story (app/page.tsx, lib/scrollStory.ts choreography kept) plus about, services, contact, faq, terms, privacy. Visitor mode: Persuade (terms/privacy are Read). Code-led build (no image generation on this machine).

## Audience, action, proof

Founders, growing businesses and creators deciding whether to start a conversation. Action: the enquiry form on /contact/ that composes an email to hello@trayang.com; every "Plan your growth" leads there. Proof: client copy only; no invented clients, numbers or testimonials (hidden slots in data/site.ts).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Strategy, creativity and execution are three ink passes; where they register, the brand appears. Refuses the dark-luxury agency template of centred headline, gold gradient text and an equal card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Screenprint on two stocks. Cream mulberry paper (#E8DDC5) and brown kraft board (#3B2112); inks are brown, gold #D89B52 and light gold #F0C27A, overlapping by multiply on cream and screen on brown, with paper fibre and ink grit. Big Shoulders Stencil caps for display, Archivo for text, narrow tracked Archivo caps for labels. Registration crosses at sheet corners, double-rule dividers with a diamond ornament, solid ink buttons flanked by diamond marks, ruled panels instead of cards.

STORY: The visitor sees what Trayang does in one line and four pillars, watches the passes register into one purpose, steps through ten capabilities, understands the joined-up approach, and ends at a clear enquiry.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Brown printed header band. Cream sheet with corner registration crosses; left two thirds the stacked stencil headline "WE BUILD BRANDS. / WE MOVE BUSINESSES FORWARD." at ~7rem in brown and gold, its ghost passes sliding into register; under it a diamond rule, one sentence and the primary ink button "Plan your growth" plus "Explore services". Right third the team-at-the-moodboard photo printed as a brown/gold duotone with an offset light-gold ghost pass. A ruled bottom band holds the four pillars, each with an overlap glyph.

FORM: Registered Overprint (challenger ink-pigment-printmaking-screenprint-transparent-overlap), chosen by the user over my list's assigned #6 (brownline drawing set). seed:4b66ee81910328c2. Signature interaction: misregistered passes sliding into register (hero load, section headings, scroll-out drift). Motion grammar: strong ease-out, transform/opacity/clip only, press scale 0.97.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
